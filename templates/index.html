Workflow

How SENTINEL
Works.

01

Reporter Logs In

Any student or staff member opens SENTINEL on their phone or desktop, logs in as a Reporter, and reaches the incident form.

02

Submit an Incident

They enter their School ID, select a category (Medical, Fire, Security, Accident), drop a pin on the campus map, and optionally describe what's happening.

03

Instant Alert Fires

The report transmits in real time. The dashboard receives it immediately, triggers an audio alert, and displays the incident as active.

04

Admin Takes Command

Authorized personnel view full incident details on the dashboard, coordinate a response, and mark the incident Resolved when handled.

05

Archived for Review

Resolved incidents move automatically to Incident History, where they are stored in chronological order for future audits and analysis.
